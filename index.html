FAQ

Common questions

What do I need for an online lesson?

A laptop or tablet with a camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection. I'll send a Google Meet link with every booking. Paper and pen are fine for your working; you can hold it up or share a photo.

When are lessons available?

Weekday evenings and weekends (Melbourne/Sydney time). You'll see open times when you book the free meeting.

How do I pay?

By card through a secure Stripe link, after we've agreed a time. Packs are paid upfront.

What if I need to cancel?

Just let me know at least 24 hours before and we'll move the lesson to another time.

Do you teach students outside Victoria?

Yes. Lessons are online and I follow the VCE, HSC, SACE and QCE courses, so students anywhere in Australia are welcome.
